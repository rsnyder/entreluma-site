Entreluma is an open-source project by [Ron Snyder](https://github.com/rsnyder) for publishing stories that connect prose with images, maps, video, and sources. It is designed for people who want to explain a place, examine an image, share research, or build a visual narrative while keeping their content in Markdown.

## Where it comes from

Entreluma grew from the interactive storytelling work of [Juncture](https://labs.jstor.org/projects/juncture/) and builds on the [Chirpy Jekyll theme](https://github.com/cotes2020/chirpy-theme). The project was initially developed under the name StoryKit and renamed Entreluma to distinguish it from other products.

## Three parts, clear roles

- [Entreluma template](https://github.com/rsnyder/entreluma): create an independent site with neutral defaults and an example story.
- [This project website](https://github.com/rsnyder/entreluma-site): learn about Entreluma and explore its publishing capabilities.
- [Entreluma Editor](https://editor.entreluma.org): an optional browser application for writing, previewing, and syncing stories with GitHub.

Your published site is a static website. The editor is optional, and your stories remain in your repository.
